# DDR-0001: The Private Ledger visual language

The wallet shares the State's arms, Manrope, and the Operations palette (Night Ink `#0B1628`, Covenant
Navy `#15294A`, Ledger Parchment `#F4EEDF`, Seal Gold `#C49A4E`, Laurel `#2F6B4F`) but not the explorer's
blue dashboard. Groups are made with rules and alignment, not cards; surfaces appear only where they mean
a document (certificate, instruction, QR frame). Gold is one accent per view: the rule under the primary
action, the active index mark, seals. The wallet has its own generated terrain (`landing`, `wallet`,
`ceremony`), seeded and reproducible by `npm run generate:contours`.
