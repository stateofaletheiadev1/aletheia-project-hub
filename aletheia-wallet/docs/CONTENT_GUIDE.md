# Content guide

Calm, exact, institutional. Short sentences. Never blame the reader.

- Name the ledger **AletheiaChain**; "stablechain" only as a lowercase description. The wallet is
  **Aletheia Wallet**, descriptor *The Private Ledger*. Mainnet is shown as **Mainnet Beta**.
- Status words are fixed: *Submitted to the network*, *Accepted*, *Awaiting finality*, *Finalized at block
  N*, *Failed on chain*, *Expired before inclusion*, *Declined*. "Submitted" never means "done".
- Errors: what happened, then what to do. "Address not recognised on AletheiaChain", not "You entered a
  wrong address". Technical detail goes behind a disclosure.
- Assets: AEY is the settlement unit and its issuance is governed by the Reserve Bank of Aletheia. Never
  write that AEY is issued, redeemable, legal tender or backed. No prices, no fiat, no percentages.
- Planned features say "planned" with one sentence of what they will do. No "coming soon".
- Security copy never asks for the phrase and always says no official will.
- Languages: English ships. German, Dutch, Italian and Serbian change number and date formats only, until
  reviewed translations exist; security and legal strings are never machine-translated.
