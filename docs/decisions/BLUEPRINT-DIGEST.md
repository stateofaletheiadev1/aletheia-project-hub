## 1. Constitutional & Philosophical Foundation

- **Name:** The Sovereign Covenant State of Aletheia (Short form: *Aletheia*). Demonym: *Aletheian*.
- **Etymology:** Greek *Aletheia* — truth, literally "that which is not hidden / unclosed".
- **Founding Principle:** A compact, high-efficiency maritime republic where proof is the default, not the exception. Most public friction (lost files, delayed payments, opaque spending) is eliminated through an immutable, auditable distributed ledger.
- **The Covenant:** The foundational constitutional charter binding state, citizens, and states in mutual obligation.
- **Motto:** *Veritas in Foedere* (Truth in Covenant).
- **Five Official Languages:**
  1. **English:** Primary working language of the state (Assembly debates, court records, APS standards, official code).
  2. **German:** Full official status (engineering, industry, public administration).
  3. **Dutch:** Full official status (maritime affairs, North Sea coordination, European affairs).
  4. **Serbian (Latin & Cyrillic):** Full official status (both scripts accepted across all civil records, forms, and apps).
  5. **Italian:** Full official status (culture, medical registry, Mediterranean trade).

## 3. Structure of Government

### 3.1 The Three Traditional Powers + Fourth Pillar (Verification)
1. **The Covenant Assembly (Legislature):** 61 members elected every 4 years by open-list proportional representation across the 5 territorial constituencies.
2. **The Council of the States (Senate/Territorial Chamber):** 15 members (3 appointed by each State Council and 3 by Federal District) with suspensive review on state/land/currency matters.
3. **The Executive (Chancellor & Cabinet):** Chancellor elected by the Assembly; Cabinet capped constitutionally at **nine ministries**:
   - Ministry of Treasury & Finance
   - Ministry of Digital State
   - Ministry of Security & Civil Defence
   - Ministry of Health & Social Services
   - Ministry of Education & Science
   - Ministry of Maritime Affairs & Ports
   - Ministry of Infrastructure & Energy
   - Ministry of Foreign Affairs & Justice
   - Ministry of Agriculture & Environment
4. **Independent Constitutional Bodies:**
   - Auditor-General (publishes cryptographic proofs of treasury spending every epoch)
   - Electoral Commission
   - Data Protection & Registry Ombudsman
   - Monetary Board (governing reserve invariants)
5. **The Judiciary:** High Court of Aletheia, Court of Maritime & Commercial Disputes, Appellate Chamber, and Constitutional Chamber. Hybrid dispute system with automated smart-contract arbitration for commercial micro-claims.

## 5. Economic & Monetary Architecture

- **Sovereign Ledger (StableChain):**
  - High-performance, Byzantine Fault Tolerant distributed ledger (500 ms slots, 512-slot epochs).
  - Native settlement rail for all public finance, identity verification, and land registry.
  - Zero gas speculation or volatile tokens for gas; fixed fee schedule in micro-cents.
- **Currencies:**
  - **AEY / ALUSD:** 100% reserve-backed payment stablecoin pegged 1:1 to EUR/USD basket, audited cryptographically in real-time.
  - **ALnT:** Native reserve and staking asset of the state network, strictly governed by supply invariants $I_1 - I_{12}$.
- **Aletheia Payment Network (APN):** High-throughput settlement rail connecting merchant terminals, state tax-free accounts, and cross-border SEPA/SWIFT gateways.
- **National Funding Model:**
  - **Zero personal income tax**, zero capital gains tax.
  - Revenue derived from land leases, sovereign development bonds, corporate e-residency licensing fees, and the **Covenant Endowment Fund**.

## 6. Citizenship, E-Residency & Immigration

### 6.1 Membership Tiers
1. **e-Resident:** Digital identity for non-residents. Enables remote company registration, banking, contract signing, and APN access. Conveys **no physical residency or political voting rights**.
2. **Resident (Temporary):** 5-year renewable permit for workers, entrepreneurs, scientists, students. Entitles holder to live, work, access health/education, and vote in municipal state elections after 2 years.
3. **Permanent Resident:** Conferred after 5 years continuous lawful residence, clean compliance record, and civic integration test.
4. **Citizen:** Conferred by descent or naturalisation (after PR + language proficiency in English and one other official language + solemn Covenant Oath). Full political voting and passport rights.

### 6.2 The Aletheian Passport
- Format: ICAO Doc 9303 compliant electronic machine-readable passport (eMRTD).
- Cover: **Covenant Burgundy (`#701824`)** with gold-foil arms and chip symbol.
- Five languages on all field labels (English, German, Dutch, Serbian, Italian).
- Contactless chip with status verifiable against the StableChain Identity Registry (zero PII on-chain).

## 7. Status Taxonomy (Strict Enforcement)

Every feature, institution, and project must carry exactly one status tag:
1. `Operational` — Currently active and provable today.
2. `Under Development` — Currently in active engineering or construction.
3. `Planned` — Approved in national roadmap for upcoming implementation.
4. `Proposed` — Formulated policy or design proposal awaiting legislative sanction.
5. `Vision` — Long-range aspirational horizon (2030–2045).
