# Design reference

What the public portal actually uses, taken from its **source code** (`aletheia-gov/src`) and the
review screenshots committed with it (`aletheia-gov/docs/design/review/*@1280.jpg` and `@390.jpg`,
9 October 2026). Live pages were fetched as HTML on 15 October 2026 (edition line and empty
registers confirmed). Rendered captures of the live site at 375, 768, 1280 and 1600 px are
not yet taken; they are part of the Phase 2 screenshot run.

## Colour

Generated scales in `aletheia-gov/src/styles/tokens.css` (OKLCH, from four core colours). The
ones that carry meaning:

| Token | Hex | Role on the portal |
|---|---|---|
| `navy-950` | `#0B1628` | Mastheads, footer, `theme-color`, `html` background ("the sea") |
| `navy-800` (Covenant Navy) | `#15294A` | Focus outline, links on parchment, register heading rule |
| `parchment-100` (Ledger Parchment) | `#F4EEDF` | Main reading surface ("the ledger"), with SVG paper grain |
| `parchment-50` | `#FBF8F1` | Paler ledger surface |
| `gold-500` (Seal Gold) | `#C49A4E` | Hairlines (45 % on parchment, 30 % on navy), section-mark rule, list dashes |
| `gold-700` | `#7F6026` | Gold text on parchment (section marks, status glyphs) |
| `gold-400` | `#D6AD66` | Gold text on navy (overlines) |
| `green-600` (Laurel Green) | `#2F6B4F` | Core colour; `green-700` `#23563E` for the Operational glyph |
| `ink-900` | `#141A24` | Body text on parchment; `ink-600`/`ink-700` secondary |

Documented values in the brief all match the source: `#0B1628`, `#15294A`, `#F4EEDF`,
`#C49A4E`, `#2F6B4F`. Contrast pairs were measured in `aletheia-gov/docs/design/contrast.md`.

## Type

- **Manrope** via `next/font/google`, subsets `latin` + `latin-ext`, variable, `display: swap`,
  `preload: false`. Feature settings `ss01`, `cv11`; `tabular-nums` set on `body`.
- **IBM Plex Mono** 400/500 for references and numbers (`.t-ref`, register counters).
  *Disagreement with the brief:* the brief requires Manrope only in the workbench. The workbench
  follows the brief (Manrope 600, tabular figures, +0.02em); the portal is not changed.
- Portal scale is display-led and light: display 300 at up to 9 rem; h1 400; h2 500; h3 600 at
  22 px; body 17 px / 1.65; meta 13 px, 600, caps, +0.12em. A marketing-and-reading site.
  The workbench uses the brief's denser operational scale (§9.3, 14 px UI) but keeps the
  portal's habits: light-to-medium weights for large headings rather than 800, caps overlines
  at +0.12em, `ss01`/`cv11`.
  *Disagreement:* the brief asks for h1 700. At 30 px, Manrope 700 reads heavy beside the
  portal's 400 headings. The workbench uses 600 for h1/h2 and records that in an ADR so the
  family resemblance holds.

## Grid and space

- `.shell`: max 1440 px, gutter `clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 4rem)`. `.grid-12` with
  `clamp(1rem, 2vw, 2rem)` gaps. Chapter spacing `clamp(6rem, 12vw, 12rem)` (too loose for a tool).
- Register pages: a 4-column left rail of facts (published count, reference format,
  publication rule, superseded versions) and an 8-column register with a navy 1 px rule under
  its heading and gold hairlines between rows.

## Rules and borders

Structure is drawn with 1 px gold hairlines (`--rule-ledger`) and one heavier navy rule under
register headings. No shadows, no cards, radius effectively 0.

## Components observed

- **Section mark:** 48 px gold hairline, then `§ 01` in gold-700 caps meta, then a label in ink-600.
  Registers use `§ R1`.
- **Status seal:** 14 px circular glyph, a distinct shape for each status (dashed, ring, half,
  three-quarters, dot, full, check, slash, bar), plus a caps label. Never colour alone.
- **Reference:** Plex Mono 13 px. Empty registers show three ghost rows `AGV-DOC-····-····` with
  fading hairlines, then a bold sentence and a plain explanation.
- **Links:** underline in gold hairline that darkens to navy on hover; "draw-link" underline
  that grows from the left; arrow nudges 4 px.
- **Footer colophon:** arms, gold caps "SOVEREIGN COVENANT STATE OF ALETHEIA", motto set large and
  upright, founding-stage notice with a small diamond marker, four link columns, edition line
  ("Edition of 15 October 2026 · No records published yet · Capital (proposed): Antioch").

## Motion

Ease `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` ("tide") and standard `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`;
180 ms quick, 320 ms base, long reveal and hero animations for the public site. Everything is
reduced to opacity or nothing under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The workbench keeps the curves and
drops the reveals: 120–200 ms state changes only.

## Voice

British English ("organisation", "programme"). Sentence case headings. Short declarative
sentences. Absence is stated plainly with the reason: "No documents have been published yet.
The National Blueprint … will be published here as versioned documents once they have passed
review." Founding-stage honesty in every footer.
*Observed inconsistency:* the footer says "recognized" (US spelling). Recorded only; the
portal is not changed by this project.
