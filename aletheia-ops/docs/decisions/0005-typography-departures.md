# ADR 0005: Typography departures from the brief and the portal

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-15

## Decision

- **Manrope only.** The portal sets references in IBM Plex Mono. The workbench follows the brief:
  Manrope 600, tabular figures, +0.02em. The portal is not changed.
- **Heading weights.** The brief proposes 700 for h1 and h2. The portal sets large headings at
  300–500. At workbench sizes (30 px and 22 px) Manrope 700 looks heavy next to the portal, so
  h1 and h2 use 600 and h3 600. The display line on the briefing uses 500. 700 is kept for
  overlines.
- `ss01` and `cv11` stylistic sets as on the portal.
