# Open questions

Nothing listed here will be invented. Until a question is answered, the site ships an honest pending state.

**Blocking** means a decision is needed before the phase named. Everything else proceeds on the default shown.

## Decisions (2026-10-19, Emeka Iwuagwu)

- **Q2 Name:** "Reserve Bank of Aletheia" on this site.
- **Q4 Banknotes:** publish the current (revised) artwork with a visible "SPECIMEN · design concept" overprint and a caption saying the inscriptions are design text, not statements of fact.
- **Q12 CI:** a job in the root `.gitlab-ci.yml` (the one file outside `aletheia-rba/`, approved).
- **Delivery:** continue through the phases, committing and reporting per phase; stop only for blocking decisions.
